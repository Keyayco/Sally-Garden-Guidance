Process

A clear path from vision to finish.

Premium results need calm structure. Our process keeps decisions crisp, budgets controlled, and timelines predictable—without sacrificing design depth.

What to expect

Each step is designed to reduce uncertainty and keep your project moving.

01 Consultation

Site visit + aesthetic direction

We map constraints, sun, privacy, circulation, and the architectural style of your home. Then we define a visual direction with material and planting references.

02 Design

Plans, palettes, and a build-ready scope

We develop layouts, levels, material specs, and planting schedules. You get clarity: what’s included, what’s optional, and why each element is there.

03 Build

Crafted execution with tight staging

Clean site management, precise finishes, and thoughtful sequencing so the space comes alive quickly without the chaos.

04 Care

Seasonal tuning and long-term performance

Your garden matures. We keep it intentional with pruning, irrigation checks, re-planting, and upgrades as seasons shift.
