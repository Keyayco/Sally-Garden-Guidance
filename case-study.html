Case study

Stone, water, and a quiet arrival sequence.

A compact courtyard re-shaped into three zones: arrival, dining terrace, and an evening garden with warm, discreet uplighting.

Project snapshot
  • ScopeDesign + build + planting
  • Timeline6 weeks
  • ZonesArrival, dining, evening
  • SignatureWarm stone + water
Request a consult See more work
Design intent

Calm geometry, rich texture, and a lighting plan that turns evenings into a scene—without visual noise.

Stonework Reflective water Native texture Warm light

Challenge

The courtyard felt fragmented: hard edges, unclear circulation, and planting that didn’t support privacy. Night lighting was harsh, so the space disappeared after sunset.

Approach

We re-composed the courtyard as a sequence: a quiet arrival plane, a dining terrace aligned to interior sight-lines, and a lush edge that frames the space. Materials were reduced to a warm stone family to keep the architecture feeling clean.

Luxury is restraint: fewer materials, better details, and a planting palette that reads as texture—not chaos.

Planting & lighting

Planting was layered for longevity: structural evergreens, soft mid-height texture, and seasonal movement. A warm, discreet lighting plan added depth and safety without glare—uplighting trees, washing stone, and outlining paths.
